Disclaimer
Citizenship by merit is granted at the discretion of the Minister responsible for citizenship, on the recommendation of an independent Evaluation Board, under the Maltese Citizenship Act (Cap. 188) and Subsidiary Legislation 188.06. Every application is assessed individually, and outcomes are not guaranteed. Fees and contributions vary by case and are confirmed in your individual fee proposal. This roadmap is for general information only and is not legal advice.
