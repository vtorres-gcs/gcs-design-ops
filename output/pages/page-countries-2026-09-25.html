Programme Overview: Citizenship and Residency Options
An increasing number of countries offer residency and citizenship programmes giving families and individuals the optionality to secure a plan B tailored to their needs. These programmes fall under two broad categories: Citizenship Planning and Residency Planning, which grant citizenship or residency based on investment in the country or exceptional contribution and achievement. Residency can also be secured through income, rather than investment — whether passive or active income. Below you will find the complete list of residency and citizenship options Global Citizen Solutions offers our clients.
