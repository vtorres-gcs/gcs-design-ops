Key Facts And Options
✓ Minimum qualifying investment from EUR 150,000 (contribution) plus a property route.
✓ Visa-free travel across the Schengen Area for the main applicant and dependants.
✓ No minimum stay requirement to maintain residency status.
✓ Processing time of approximately 4 to 6 months from submission.
✓ Dependants include spouse, children, and qualifying parents and grandparents.

The Malta Permanent Residence Programme (MPRP) grants non-EU nationals and their families the right to live, work and study in Malta indefinitely, with visa-free access to the Schengen Area. It is one of the few EU residency-by-investment routes that grants permanent — rather than temporary — status from the outset.

Malta Permanent Residence Program MPRP

Administered by Malta's Residency Malta Agency, the MPRP was relaunched with revised contribution thresholds and due-diligence requirements. Applicants must satisfy a capital and property test, contribute to the National Development and Social Fund, and pass a stringent background-check process before a certificate is issued.

Image — Valletta harbour and fortifications

Because the programme grants permanent residence rather than a renewable permit, successful applicants are not required to reapply or demonstrate continued eligibility once the certificate is issued, subject to maintaining the qualifying property and fund contribution.

Who Is The Malta Golden Visa Best Suited For?

— Globally-mobile families seeking a Plan B within the EU without a relocation requirement.
— Investors who value permanent status over a renewable permit.
— Applicants prioritising a stable, English-speaking EU jurisdiction with a straightforward legal system.
— Families wanting to include parents and grandparents on a single application.

Malta Permanent Residency Program Requirements 2026

— Be a non-EU, non-EEA and non-Swiss national, at least 18 years of age.
— Show proof of stable and regular resources of at least EUR 500,000, of which EUR 150,000 must be financial assets.
— Hold a qualifying property, purchased or rented, meeting the minimum thresholds.
— Make a non-refundable contribution to the National Development and Social Fund.
— Hold valid health insurance covering Malta and the EU, and pass due-diligence checks.

Malta Permanent Residence And Tourism

Image — Malta coastline and tourist promenade

Beyond residency, Malta's tourism infrastructure, climate and connectivity make it an attractive base for holders looking to divide time between the island and their home country, without the minimum-stay obligations found in comparable programmes.

Is Citizenship Possible Through The Malta Golden Visa?

The MPRP does not itself lead to citizenship. Malta operates a separate route, the Citizenship by Naturalisation for Exceptional Services by Direct Investment, for applicants seeking a Maltese passport. Long-term MPRP holders may explore standard naturalisation after meeting residence-based requirements. Learn more on our Malta Citizenship page.
