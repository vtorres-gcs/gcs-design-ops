04 — Investment Routes
Real Estate Routes Linked
to Malta Programmes
1
MPRP qualifying purchase
Purchase a qualifying property (min. EUR 375,000 in Malta or EUR 300,000 in Gozo/South) to satisfy the Malta Permanent Residency Programme's property condition.
2
MPRP qualifying rental
Alternatively, rent a qualifying property (min. EUR 14,000/yr in Malta or EUR 10,000/yr in Gozo/South) for the duration of the holding period.
3
Minimum holding period
Qualifying property must be retained for a minimum of five years from the date the residency card is issued.
4
Independent valuation
All qualifying property is subject to an independent valuation to confirm it meets the applicable investment threshold.
5
Ownership structure
Property may be held individually or jointly with a spouse or civil partner included in the same application.
