02 — Country Information
Country Information
🏛️
Government & Politics
Malta is a parliamentary republic with a stable democratic tradition. It is a member of the EU, NATO's Partnership for Peace, and the Commonwealth.
💰
Economy
A services-driven economy anchored by financial services, tourism, and digital industries. Malta has one of the EU's lowest unemployment rates.
📚
Education & Healthcare
Free public education and healthcare for residents. Home to the University of Malta, one of the oldest universities in the world.
🌤️
Climate & Lifestyle
Mediterranean climate with over 300 sunny days per year. Known for a relaxed, high-quality lifestyle with excellent connectivity to Europe.
✈️
Connectivity
Malta International Airport connects to 100+ destinations. Close proximity to major European hubs — 3 hours from London, 2 from Rome.
⚖️
Legal System
Common law tradition blended with EU law. English is an official language of the courts. Malta scores highly on rule-of-law indices.
