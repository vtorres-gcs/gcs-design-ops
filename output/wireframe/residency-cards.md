## Europe — Golden Visa & Investor Residency

### Cyprus — Golden Visa

The Cyprus Golden Visa grants investors permanent residency for a minimum EUR 300,000 investment in real estate, business, or funds.

- **Investment from:** EUR 300,000

### Greece — Golden Visa

Under the Greece Golden Visa Program, a minimum real estate investment starting from EUR 250,000 grants investors a permanent residence permit with no minimum stay requirement.

- **Investment from:** EUR 250,000
- **CTA:** Learn more →

### Greece — Passive Income Residency

Applicants can qualify through monthly passive income or through a lump-sum bank deposit as an alternative. Requires relocation to Greece and offers an option for reduced flat tax rate on foreign pension income for qualifying retirees.

- **Income from:** EUR 3,500/month
- **CTA:** Learn more →

### Greece — Digital Nomad Residency

Suited to remote professionals working for employers or clients based outside Greece. Among the fastest visa approvals in Europe, requires relocation to Greece.

- **Income from:** EUR 3,500/month
- **CTA:** Learn more →

### Hungary — Hungary Golden Visa

Hungary Golden Visa from EUR 250,000 in an approved real estate fund. Best for investors who want the EU's most affordable Golden Visa alongside a 10 year residency permit with no minimum stay requirement.

### Italy — Investor Visa

### Portugal — Golden Visa

### United States — EB-5 Investor Visa

Provides a direct route to a US green card without requiring active, day-to-day management of the business. Most applicants invest through a Regional Center, with the reduced $800,000 threshold available in Targeted Employment Areas.

### United States — E-2 Investor Visa

Has no fixed minimum investment — the amount only needs to be "substantial" relative to the business itself, giving investors flexibility in scale. Available to nationals of treaty countries and their families, with renewals possible indefinitely.
